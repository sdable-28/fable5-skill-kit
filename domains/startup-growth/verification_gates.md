# Startup Growth — Verification Gates

Domain-specific pass/fail gates. Run before delivering any growth diagnosis or recommendation.

### Gate 1 — PMF Two-Signal Confirmation
- **Pass:** PMF is claimed only when Sean Ellis ≥40% "very disappointed" AND the retention curve flattens.
- **Fail:** PMF asserted on one signal, or on "some people use it."
- **Check:** Confirm both artifacts exist and both pass their threshold.

### Gate 2 — Retention Curve Flattens Before Scale
- **Pass:** Acquisition-scaling recommendations only follow a cohort retention curve with a flat tail above 0%.
- **Fail:** Spend increases proposed while the curve still decays toward zero.
- **Check:** Inspect the cohort retention chart for a horizontal asymptote before endorsing scale.

### Gate 3 — Cohorted, Never Blended
- **Pass:** Retention, CAC, and NRR are reported by cohort and segment.
- **Fail:** A single company-wide blended number is used for any of these.
- **Check:** Confirm no headline metric is an all-users average.

### Gate 4 — Stage-Match
- **Pass:** Every recommendation names the ARR band (0→$1M / $1M→$10M / $10M→$100M) and the tactic belongs to it.
- **Fail:** A wrong-stage playbook (e.g. Series B outbound on a Series A self-serve product).
- **Check:** Confirm each recommendation states its stage and the tactic fits it.

### Gate 5 — NRR as First-Class Metric
- **Pass:** NRR/NDR is computed and trended alongside new MRR; below-100% is flagged.
- **Fail:** New MRR reported with no net-revenue-retention line beside it.
- **Check:** Confirm NRR is present and trended, not just gross new MRR.

### Gate 6 — Vanity Metric Strip
- **Pass:** Every success claim is activated-and-retained users or dollars.
- **Fail:** A cumulative gross count (signups, impressions, downloads) used as success.
- **Check:** Scan claims — reject any resting on a cumulative gross count.

### Gate 7 — CAC Paired With Retention
- **Pass:** Every CAC figure is reported with the same cohort's retention.
- **Fail:** Cheap CAC celebrated without checking the cohort retains.
- **Check:** Confirm CAC and retention appear for the same segment.

### Gate 8 — Growth Model Justified by ACV/Buyer
- **Pass:** The chosen growth model (PLG/SLG/community/marketing/partnership) cites ACV band and buyer type.
- **Fail:** A model chosen by fashion with no ACV/buyer rationale.
- **Check:** Confirm the model choice names its ACV and buyer basis.

### Gate 9 — Loop Named or Funnel Admitted
- **Pass:** Each growth proposal names its compounding loop and cycle time, or explicitly admits it's a non-compounding funnel push.
- **Fail:** Linear acquisition presented as if it compounds.
- **Check:** Confirm a loop diagram/cycle-time exists or the funnel-nature is stated.

### Gate 10 — K-Factor Full Math
- **Pass:** Any virality/K-factor claim shows invites-sent × conversion-rate × cycle time.
- **Fail:** A viral coefficient asserted without both terms and the time constant.
- **Check:** Confirm both loop terms and the cycle time are present; treat unmeasured virality as zero.

### Gate 11 — One Channel to Saturation
- **Pass:** A second channel is proposed only after the first's saturation and attribution are understood.
- **Fail:** Multiple new channels started simultaneously, corrupting attribution.
- **Check:** Confirm primary-channel saturation evidence precedes any second-channel proposal.

### Gate 12 — Timing vs. Execution Attributed
- **Pass:** Every growth post-mortem assigns "it didn't work" to market/channel timing or to execution, with evidence.
- **Fail:** Failure blamed on effort without checking for external channel/market shift.
- **Check:** Confirm the post-mortem separates timing from execution before assigning cause.
