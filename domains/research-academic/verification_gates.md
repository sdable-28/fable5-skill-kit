# Research & Academic Writing — Verification Gates

Domain-specific pass/fail gates. Run before delivering any research analysis, review, or manuscript.

### Gate 1 — Hypothesis Pre-Registration
- **Pass:** The hypothesis is stated before results; post-hoc findings are labeled exploratory.
- **Fail:** A hypothesis is presented as confirmed by the data that generated it (HARKing).
- **Check:** Confirm the hypothesis predates the analysis, or exploratory findings are demoted.

### Gate 2 — Source Hierarchy Enforced
- **Pass:** Claims rest on primary sources at the correct evidence level; secondary sources are labeled as such.
- **Fail:** A treatise or blog is cited where the primary study or statute is required.
- **Check:** Confirm each load-bearing claim cites primary evidence of appropriate rank.

### Gate 3 — Literature Search Reproducibility
- **Pass:** Systematic reviews report databases, search strings, and inclusion/exclusion per PRISMA.
- **Fail:** A "review" with an unstated, unrepeatable search.
- **Check:** Confirm the search is documented well enough to reproduce.

### Gate 4 — Study Design Matches Claim
- **Pass:** Causal claims rest on designs that support causation (RCT/quasi-experimental); observational data yields association only.
- **Fail:** Causation asserted from a correlational/retrospective design.
- **Check:** Confirm the claim's strength matches the design's inferential power.

### Gate 5 — Power and Sample Adequacy
- **Pass:** Quantitative claims report N and a power analysis; underpowered results are flagged.
- **Fail:** An effect declared on a sample too small to detect it.
- **Check:** Confirm the power calc exists and N supports the claimed effect size.

### Gate 6 — Multiple-Comparison Honesty
- **Pass:** Every test run is counted; corrections applied or the look-count disclosed.
- **Fail:** One significant result cherry-picked from many silent tests (p-hacking).
- **Check:** Confirm the number of comparisons is stated and handled.

### Gate 7 — Confounding Addressed
- **Pass:** Plausible confounders are identified and controlled or acknowledged as limitations.
- **Fail:** A relationship reported with obvious confounders ignored.
- **Check:** Confirm the confounders are named and either adjusted for or flagged.

### Gate 8 — Mandatory Limitations Section
- **Pass:** The deliverable includes an explicit limitations section bounding the claims.
- **Fail:** Findings presented with no stated limits, implying over-generalization.
- **Check:** Confirm a limitations section exists and constrains the conclusions.

### Gate 9 — Synthesis Over Summary
- **Pass:** The literature is integrated into an argument (agreements, tensions, gaps), not listed source-by-source.
- **Fail:** The review is a serial summary with no synthesis.
- **Check:** Confirm the text relates sources to each other, not just in sequence.

### Gate 10 — So-What Present
- **Pass:** Every finding carries an implication; the abstract states problem→method→results→implications.
- **Fail:** Results reported with no stated significance ("missing the so-what").
- **Check:** Confirm each finding has an implication and the abstract closes on implications.

### Gate 11 — Data-Viz Integrity
- **Pass:** Charts have honest axes (no truncation that distorts), minimal chartjunk, high data-ink ratio.
- **Fail:** A truncated axis or 3D effect inflates the visual claim (high lie factor).
- **Check:** Confirm axes start where they should and the visual magnitude matches the data.
