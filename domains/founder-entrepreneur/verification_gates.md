# Founder & Entrepreneur — Verification Gates

Domain-specific pass/fail gates. Run before delivering founder-facing analysis or a decision memo.

### Gate 1 — Mom-Test Compliance
- **Pass:** Validation evidence is about buyers' actual past behavior and spend, not their opinion of the idea.
- **Fail:** Evidence is "people said they'd love it" or hypothetical willingness.
- **Check:** Every validation quote references something the buyer already did/paid, not a future promise.

### Gate 2 — Market Sizing Bottoms-Up
- **Pass:** TAM/SAM/SOM is built from actual buying units × price, from the ground up.
- **Fail:** The number is a top-down slice of a Gartner/analyst figure ("1% of a $10B market").
- **Check:** Trace the market number to counted buyers × ACV; reject percentage-of-big-number.

### Gate 3 — Equity-Split Defensibility
- **Pass:** Co-founder split reflects contribution/risk and includes vesting with a cliff; deviations from equal are reasoned.
- **Fail:** Reflexive 50/50 with no vesting, or a split with no rationale.
- **Check:** Confirm vesting + cliff exist and the split has a stated basis.

### Gate 4 — IP Assignment Before Raise
- **Pass:** All founder and early-contributor IP is assigned to the entity before any fundraise step.
- **Fail:** IP assignment is missing, verbal, or deferred past a raise.
- **Check:** Confirm a signed IP assignment exists for every founder/contractor before capital.

### Gate 5 — Cap-Table Coherence
- **Pass:** The cap table sums to 100%, includes the option pool sized to the hiring plan, and models post-round dilution.
- **Fail:** Percentages don't reconcile, no option pool, or dilution unmodeled.
- **Check:** Sum ownership to 100%; confirm pool size maps to planned hires; confirm post-money dilution is shown.

### Gate 6 — Term-Sheet Signal Separation
- **Pass:** Term-sheet analysis separates economics/control/protective terms that matter from noise, and flags non-standard terms.
- **Fail:** All terms treated equally, or a control/liquidation term missed.
- **Check:** Confirm the analysis names which terms are decision-relevant and which are boilerplate.

### Gate 7 — Stage-Appropriate Instrument
- **Pass:** SAFE / convertible note / priced round choice is justified by stage and round size.
- **Fail:** The instrument is chosen by default with no stage rationale.
- **Check:** Confirm the recommendation cites stage and amount as the reason for the instrument.

### Gate 8 — First-Traction Manual Proof
- **Pass:** Early traction is manual and founder-led (first customers won by direct outreach; delivery done by hand) before any growth-system spend.
- **Fail:** The plan reaches for ads/SEO/sales hires before manual $10k-MRR proof exists.
- **Check:** Confirm the first-10-customers were won personally and delivery was manual before scaling proposals.

### Gate 9 — Investor-Interest vs. Market-Demand
- **Pass:** Demand evidence comes from paying customers, kept separate from investor enthusiasm.
- **Fail:** Investor interest is cited as proof of product-market demand.
- **Check:** Confirm the demand claim rests on customer behavior, not term-sheet count.

### Gate 10 — Survival-Metric Priority
- **Pass:** The founder decision optimizes runway/retention/revenue, not press or optics.
- **Fail:** The recommendation chases a launch splash or vanity milestone over survival.
- **Check:** Confirm the stated success metric is a survival metric (runway, retention, MRR), not coverage.
